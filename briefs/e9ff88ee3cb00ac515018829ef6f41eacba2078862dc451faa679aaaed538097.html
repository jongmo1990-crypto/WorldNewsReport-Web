BOJ Survey: GenAI Expanding Into Core Banking Ops

This is the third consecutive annual BOJ survey on AI use in Japanese finance, and the reported shift from administrative to core, customer-data-driven GenAI use -- combined with explicit central-bank attention to information-leakage and output-reliability risk -- signals a maturing, closely-watched adoption trend rather than a one-off event. Reports like this typically precede or reinforce supervisory expectations and can become de facto governance benchmarks. It remains a descriptive/study report rather than binding regulation, and the specific survey findings that would clarify the actual depth of adoption and risk severity are not present in the available evidence.

자세히 보기

① 무슨 일이 있었나

The Bank of Japan's Financial System and Bank Examination Department published (Aug 24, 2026) an annex to the Financial System Report on the use and risk management of generative AI (GenAI) by Japanese financial institutions, based on its third AI-use survey since FY2024 (targeting 150 financial institutions) plus interviews with IT vendors and financial institutions. Per the report's own text, GenAI use in Japanese financial institutions is expanding from general administrative tasks into core operations that leverage customer data, and is expected to expand and evolve further with technological advancement. The report states institutions need to understand GenAI-specific risks -- information leakage and the uncertainty of outputs and behaviors -- and enhance risk-management frameworks via a risk-based approach. The evidence excerpt is truncated exactly where the report's specific survey findings begin, so the quantitative results (adoption rates, incident data, risk-management maturity levels) are not available in this context.
